Version 1.4.11. Roy’s current authored manuscript, a white phone screen, portrait choice cards and native-size text. Phones fill the available viewport; desktop previews use a 480 × 960 canvas and scroll when needed. The description changes immediately when a card is selected. New cards arrive afterward; card motion, floating attribute amounts and updated totals finish at 860 ms. The preferred hand lingers within that same sequence. Logs show only authored prose. Signed direct changes appear immediately below their attribute totals and linger until the next task; existing accounting is preserved. One through four cards keep identical dimensions. Worn ink is the default card texture: worn inner edges and light paper grain, with solid black outer edges, white centers and solid inked tops with worn patches. Outer dimensions are unchanged. The latest pain/need markings and withdrawal rules are rolled back. Event choices use a simple symmetrical dividing fork; the repeated (Y/N) suffix is removed from the introductory questions, while Y and N remain on their choice cards. The original hand is another 25% smaller, with the click point just below its middle finger. Attributes sit closer to the title bar, with a permanent thin rule beneath the row and room for signed changes. The date and minute clock appear when play begins and stay visible below the striped bar at top right, including during intertitles and scrolling; events may take more than an hour of game time. Every card has its own synchronized arrival or withdrawal sound, and used cards have a separate disintegration sound. Arrival and withdrawal are slightly staggered within the same 860 ms sequence. Attribute and story sounds remain paused.

The separate first-night prototype includes Roy’s dreams and return-to-the-hole sequence with provisional mechanics. Its test link does not save a playthrough. The main game and prototype use separate saves. Authoring tools, history and photo research stay local. Android includes the main game and runs offline.
