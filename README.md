# Alignment

A playable, HyperCard-inspired narrative game. August 6, 1997.

[Play online](https://royschwartz.github.io/alignment/) · [Download for Android](https://royschwartz.github.io/alignment/downloads/Alignment-Stage-1.apk)

Version **1.3.0** rebuilds Stage 1 around metaphysical Hunger and the shape of ordinary days. Eating can offer pleasure or improve your Lifestyle. Only food thrown into the hole relieves the Hunger. High Hunger diminishes pleasure and sharpens Disquiet; Disquiet takes Rapture throughout the day.

Morning. Work. Evening. Night. Early grocery shifts are compulsory. Dreams and the day's account close the night. Three optional world screens let you inspect your life and set intentions for future opportunities without spending time. At zero Rapture, a counted reset lets you continue with your relationships, skills, money, and obligations intact.

The return becomes a pact. The appetite grows. Carrying food becomes a burden; a wagon, friends, and paid deliveries help. Learning and a fictional stock breakthrough open another life. A tested trader and reliable care let time stretch. Closeness to PERSON requires a life that can sustain it.

The chapter contains 109 activities, 541 rotating passages, 17 intertitles, 20 night dreams, and 39 mandatory ethical events with 117 responses. Responses shape a hidden personality that affects task outcomes and emotional consequences. Once a story event appears, you must answer it. The optional house introduces MADAME, THE PRIESTESS, THE FOOL, and THE SUN, with distinct relationships, boundaries, and clearly priced company.

The prose is intensely elliptical: concrete details, short fragments, things left unsaid. Rapture sacrifices have thorns. Appealing opportunities flash; reduced motion uses static marks. Attributes arrive through intertitles and explain themselves in bold when tapped. The hole has no eyes.

Stage 1 ends with the relationship, the disturbing dream, and the Stage 2 entry. Later chapters have six-month, two-year, and eight-year timeline targets; their full campaigns are not included in this release.

The log expands. Progress saves on this device, and earlier campaigns migrate with an original-save backup. Reading, inspecting the world, and closing the game do not advance time. The matching Android app works offline on Android 9 or later. Browser saves and Android saves are separate.

This repository contains the playable static build. GitHub Pages publishes the root of `main`; no build step or external service is required. To preview locally, serve this folder with any static web server.

Original historical IconStack images and their provenance/notice remain under `icons/`. Their original terms remain applicable. No additional license is granted for the game writing or code.
