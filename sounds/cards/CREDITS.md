# Card sounds

Recorded card foley from [Kenney Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), CC0 1.0. Arrival uses card-shuffle.ogg; withdrawal uses card-shove-1.ogg. The source license is included. Edits and hashes are recorded in provenance.json; tools/prepare-card-sounds.py reproduces the WAVs. Selected for card movement at Roy’s request on September 24. Attribute and story sounds remain paused.
