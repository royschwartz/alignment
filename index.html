About this stack

The complete offline first chapter of Alignment in the original HyperCard-inspired card renderer. Feed your friend, learn, begin a fintech career, and meet PERSON. Reading cards takes no time. Progress saves on this device.

The classic visual effects are new implementations inspired by HyperCard. This is not a Macintosh emulator or a HyperTalk interpreter.

Historical icons: IconStack 1.7.6 (1993), compiled by Geoff Williams and Dan Lyons. Commercial-game reuse is unverified. Source references and the original notice are included with this prototype.
