# Card sounds

Recorded foley from [Kenney Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), CC0 1.0. Each arriving card uses a short card-slide-4 flick; each withdrawing card uses card-shove-1; a disintegrating card uses a dry excerpt of cards-pack-open-2. The source license is included. Edits and hashes are recorded in provenance.json; tools/prepare-card-sounds.py reproduces the WAVs. Selected for the per-card movement requested by Roy on September 24. Attribute and story sounds remain paused.
