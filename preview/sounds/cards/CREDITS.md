# Card sounds

Four distinct arrival swooshes combine original synthesized air sweeps with recorded paper foley from [Kenney Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), CC0 1.0. The original synthesized layer is also CC0 1.0. Each arriving card uses the next swoosh, one every 250 ms. Withdrawal keeps card-shove-1; disintegration keeps cards-pack-open-2. The source license is included. Edits, synthesis seeds and hashes are in provenance.json; tools/prepare-card-sounds.py reproduces the WAVs. Attribute and story sounds remain paused.
